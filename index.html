FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Which databases can I connect to?

PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, SQL Server, Oracle, SQLite, DuckDB, MongoDB and Redis today. More sources, including cloud warehouses, are on the way.

Can it replace my current database client?

For day-to-day browsing, querying and exporting on the supported databases, yes. Some advanced features, like SSH tunnels, aren’t in the beta yet.

Does my data ever leave my computer?

No, except when you use AI. Then schema details and your question go to the provider you configured, and follow-ups don’t include prior result rows. Use Ollama to keep everything on-device.

Can the AI change my data?

No. AI-generated queries are bounded reads. Writes only happen from the SQL editor, after you review them.

How does the MCP server work?

Enable it in Settings and point your AI tool at the local endpoint. It listens on localhost only, is read-only unless you enable writes, and switches off each time rowwise opens.

Do I need my own AI key?

For AI features, yes. Use OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local model through Ollama. Everything else works without one.

How much does it cost?

Every download starts a 14-day free trial with no account. After that, a one-time license unlocks everything. AI usage is billed by your own provider.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS and Windows.
