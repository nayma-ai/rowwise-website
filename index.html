FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can rowwise replace my current database client?

For everyday browsing, querying, editing rows, schema changes, exports and imports on the supported databases, yes, including connections through SSH tunnels. It’s a beta, so tell us what you miss.

Does my data ever leave my computer?

Your rows never do. When you use AI, your question, the schema (table and column names, types and relationships; for Redis, key names) and your saved definitions go to the provider you configured. Query results are never sent. Use Ollama to keep even that on your machine.

Can the AI change my data?

Not on its own. Ask only writes read-only queries. In the SQL editor the AI can draft a change, but nothing runs until you review and confirm it.

Do I need my own AI key?

Only for AI features. Use OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local model through Ollama. Everything else works without one.

What happens when the trial ends?

rowwise asks for a license key before it opens your workspace again. Your connections, saved queries and history stay on your computer and come back as soon as you activate. There is no account to create and nothing to cancel.

Is it a subscription?

Personal and Pro are not. You pay once and keep the version you buy forever, with a year of updates included. After that you can renew updates at a discount, or keep using what you have. Buy during the beta and your updates never expire. Team licenses are billed yearly per seat.

Which databases are in preview?

Snowflake and BigQuery work today and are still being validated against more account setups. Everything else listed is fully supported.

How does the MCP server work?

Enable it in Settings and point your AI tool at the local address. It listens on localhost only, is read-only unless you enable writes, and switches off each time rowwise starts.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS (one download for Apple silicon and Intel Macs) and Windows.

Will my computer warn me when I open rowwise?

Not on a Mac: rowwise is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. The Windows beta is not yet code-signed, so Windows may show a SmartScreen notice the first time; click More info, then Run anyway.

Who makes rowwise, and how do I get help?

rowwise is built by NAYMA. Email support@nayma.ai for help, licensing or Team questions.
